### 👋 About me

I work at the intersection of **technology governance** and **applied AI engineering** inside a public-sector institution. My day-to-day lives in three places:

- 🏛️ **Architecture governance** — I help steward **MGAT** (*Marco de Gobierno de Arquitectura Tecnológica*), writing ADRs, classifying technologies, and keeping the reference architecture coherent as new tools and patterns show up.
- 🤖 **AI-assisted engineering** — I build tooling around Claude (MCP servers, skills, automations) to connect institutional systems and GraphQL APIs to AI workflows, and to turn manual processes into repeatable ones.
- 👥 **Process & team development** — I document processes under ITIL 4 / BPMN / RACI, run license and resource tracking, and work on team dynamics (DISC) for a technical team.

🌎 Based in **Aguascalientes, México** · 🗣️ Spanish & English
